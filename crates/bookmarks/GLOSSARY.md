# Bookmarks

The Context for saved web pages: some kept only so as not to forget them, others kept with their readable text to read and mark up. App-wide terms (Context, Item, Item ID, Delete, Tag, Device, Cloud Backing, Sync, Reading Progress, Markup, Highlight, Comment, Highlight Colors) are in the [App glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## Language

**Bookmark**:
One saved web page: its address, and its Article if it has one.
_Avoid_: Link (that is a reference between Items), favourite, clip

**Article**:
The readable text of a web page and its images, kept with its Bookmark when the page is Saved, without the page's menus, ads and layout. Someone else's writing: never edited, only marked up.
_Avoid_: Snapshot, copy, archive, content, clip

**Save**:
Turning a web page into a Bookmark, with or without an Article: the page as the user sees it in their browser, or as downloaded when only its Address is given. Saving an Address again opens its Bookmark.
_Avoid_: Archive (Pocket's word for "done reading"), clip, add, import

**Address**:
Where a Bookmark's page is on the web, without its tracking parameters and `#` part; no two Bookmarks share one.
_Avoid_: URL, link

**Add Article**:
Giving a Bookmark without an Article one, from its page as it is now.
_Avoid_: Fetch, refresh, download

**Drop Article**:
Taking a Bookmark's Article away with its Markup and Reading Progress, keeping the Bookmark.
_Avoid_: Delete (that is the whole Bookmark), remove

**Open Page**:
Opening a Bookmark's Address in the system's browser.
_Avoid_: Visit, open link

### Details

**Title**:
The name shown for a Bookmark: the page's title when Saved, until the user renames it.
_Avoid_: Name, headline

**Site**:
The name of the website a Bookmark's page belongs to, or its domain if it gives none.
_Avoid_: Domain, host, source

**Cover**:
The preview image a page names for itself, shown with its Bookmark.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, preview, og:image

**Excerpt**:
A short summary of the page, as the page gives it or from its Article's start.
_Avoid_: Description, summary

**Author**:
Who wrote the page, if it says.
_Avoid_: Byline, creator

**Published**:
When the page says it was published, if it does.
_Avoid_: Date (unqualified)

**Saved**:
When the Bookmark was Saved; the list shows the newest first.
_Avoid_: Added, created

**Bookmark Details**:
The view of one Bookmark's details, Tags and actions; where a Link to a Bookmark leads.
_Avoid_: Info, properties, sheet, inspector

### Reading

**Reader**:
The view in which an Article is read and marked up, in the app's own text styling.
_Avoid_: Reading mode, viewer, web view

**Last Read**:
When the Reader last showed an Article.
_Avoid_: Last opened

**Immersive Mode**:
A state of the Reader in which everything except the Article is hidden.
_Avoid_: Reading mode, focus mode, full screen

### Search

**Search**:
Finding Bookmarks by words in their Title, Site, Address or Article, ignoring case and accents; Title matches first.
_Avoid_: Filter, find, Article Search

**Article Search**:
Finding text inside the one Article open in the Reader.
_Avoid_: Search (unqualified), find
